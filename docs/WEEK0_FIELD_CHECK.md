# Week-0 User & Cyber-Café Reality Check (`T-005`)

`Goal:` Watch **3–5 real Nigerian applicants and/or cyber-café operators** solve an exact portal photo task *before* building KiloCrop's UI, to test **H1 (switching pain)**, **H2 (unprompted privacy concern)**, **H3 (gaps in `imresizer.com` / `pi7.org`)**, and the **multi-photo 4×6 café sheet** concept.

---

## 1. Applicant Task Script (10 minutes per person)

Hand the participant a **3–5 MB smartphone portrait** (NOT already cropped) on a phone and read verbatim:

> *"Pretend a school or NYSC portal just rejected your passport photo and said: **'Photo must be JPG, 600×600 px, max 25 KB'** (or **'NYSC: 200×200 px, 7–15 KB'**). Show me how you would normally fix this on your phone—or try `imresizer.com` / `pi7.org` if you don't have a favorite tool. I won't help unless you get completely stuck."*

Start a timer silently. Note every tap, hesitation, ad misclick, or abandonment.

### Non-leading debrief questions (ask *after* they finish or give up)
1. *"What annoyed you or felt hardest during that?"*
2. *"Would you trust the output file to upload straight to your portal? Why or why not?"* *(Do **not** say the word "privacy" or "upload to server" first—note if they ever mention it unprompted.)*
3. *"What happened the last time you had to upload a passport photo or signature/certificate?"* *(Check whether certificate PDFs or passport photos hurt more.)*

---

## 2. Cyber-Café Operator Check (5 minutes, 1–2 operators)

Ask a campus/neighborhood café operator:
1. *"When a student brings a WhatsApp photo for Post-UTME, NYSC, or NIS, what tool do you use right now and how many seconds/minutes does it take?"*
2. *"If a free webpage let you crop 4–8 different customers' passports onto one 4×6 print sheet with tiny initials under each cut (even offline), would you use it or is Photoshop faster?"*

## 4. Decision Gate

- **Proceed to Week 1 as planned** if ≥ 2/5 struggle, exceed ~90 s, hit misleading ads, crop wrong aspect ratios, fail `min`/`max` KB, or express clear frustration (or cafés want the offline multi-photo sheet).
- **Kill / Pivot (`TODO.md` Kill Criterion #1)** if **≥ 4 of 5 applicants finish cleanly in < 60 s** on `imresizer.com` or `pi7.org` with exact dimensions + KB and zero frustration.
